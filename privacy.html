Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how the current CVMate AI website handles information you enter while creating a CV. CVMate AI currently provides a browser-based CV builder. It does not currently offer user accounts, cloud CV storage, or an active premium payment service.

1. Information you enter

You may enter information such as your name, phone number, email address, location, education, work history, skills, certificates, languages and job preferences. This information is used in your browser to prepare the CV preview and PDF download.

2. Where your CV information goes

In the current version of CVMate AI, the answers you enter are handled by the website code in your browser. The website does not send those answers to a CVMate AI server, and it does not provide an account or cloud-storage feature. Your answers are not intentionally saved by CVMate AI after you close or reload the page. If you want to keep your CV, download the PDF to your own device.

3. Third-party services

The website loads the html2pdf library from cdnjs, a third-party content delivery service, to create PDF downloads. Your browser makes a request to that service to load the library. The library is used to turn the CV preview into a PDF; CVMate AI does not intentionally send your form answers to cdnjs. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and the CDN provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs under their own privacy policies.

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

4. Cookies, analytics and accounts

The current CVMate AI code does not intentionally set CVMate AI cookies, use analytics tracking, or require registration. This may change if new features or service providers are added. This statement does not cover cookies or technical processing that may be used by the hosting provider or third-party services.

5. Your choices and safety

  • Only enter details you are comfortable including in a CV.
  • Check your CV carefully before sharing it with an employer.
  • Download your PDF to a device you control, and avoid using a shared device if possible.
  • Do not enter passwords, bank details, identity-document numbers or other information that is not needed for a CV.

6. Children

CVMate AI is intended for people preparing job applications. If you are under 18, use it with the guidance of a parent or guardian and do not share unnecessary personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

A dedicated privacy contact address has not yet been published. We will add a direct contact method before introducing accounts, cloud storage or payment features. Please do not post personal CV information publicly when seeking support.
